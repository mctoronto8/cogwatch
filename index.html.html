Our
Mission

Listening to What the Noise is Hiding

Like the Sirens of old, influence operations sing before they strike. CogWatch exists to hear what others miss — mapping adversarial tactics in the information environment before they reshape public reality.

Modelled on the MITRE ATT&CK framework, CogWatch brings structured threat analysis to cognitive warfare — giving analysts, researchers, and institutions a common language for an uncommon threat.

Independent. Not-for-profit. Canadian.

I
Surface — Identify

Map adversarial tactics, techniques, and procedures in the cognitive domain using structured, reproducible intelligence methodology.

II
Current — Analyse

Apply operational analysis to media ecosystems, influence networks, and emerging threat vectors as they develop.

III
Depth — Defend

Equip institutions, analysts, and practitioners with frameworks and tools to recognize and counter cognitive attacks.
